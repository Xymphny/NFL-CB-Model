How a rating becomes a spread

home margin = home − away + 2.0 Thunder @ Spurs on opening night: 5.5 − 5.1 + 2.0 = Spurs by 2.4. The outcome is priced with a standard deviation of 14.2 points around that margin.

Why every team starts at half

Each season opens at half of last season's final rating (Spurs 10.9 → 5.5), and then every game moves both teams by 5% of the model's miss. That's the procedure graded at t = +5.96 on a season it had never seen.

What a rating doesn't know

Roster moves, injuries and minutes. A team that changed over the summer carries last season's number until results move it. The Players tab is where you weigh that part yourself.
